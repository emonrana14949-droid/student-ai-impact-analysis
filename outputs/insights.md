# Key Insights — Student AI Impact Analysis

> This project uses synthetic practice data generated with NumPy. The patterns below are for learning the workflow only; they are not real research findings.

- Dataset size: **500 students** and **15 columns** after feature engineering.
- The AI tool group with the highest average final score is **Claude** (74.88).
- The department with the highest average final score is **Architecture** (75.17).
- The strongest average final score appears in the **Moderate** AI usage level (76.85).
- Correlation between AI usage hours and final score: **0.09**.
- Correlation between AI usage hours and creativity score: **0.45**.
- Correlation between attendance and final score: **0.22**.
